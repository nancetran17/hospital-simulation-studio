      Autonomous Hospital · operating floor

      One surgery, played two ways at the same time.

      This is a surgery center with four operating rooms, a clean supply room in the middle, and sterile processing next to it. We follow one gallbladder surgery in OR 2, from check-in to the room being ready for the next patient. The left side is how it works today. The right side is the same case with ceiling cameras and software keeping the record, checking the counts and calling the room reset, and carriers on a ceiling rail moving instrument trays.
